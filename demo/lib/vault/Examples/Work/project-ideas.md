# Project ideas

Loose concepts live here until one has enough evidence and energy to become a project. Each idea has a small next step so the list can produce learning rather than grow indefinitely.

## Cedar Room open studio

A weekly, low-noise workspace where neighbors can read, repair small objects, or work alongside one another. The room should support participation without requiring conversation.

**Why it may matter:** Many public activities assume constant social interaction. A quieter format could welcome people who enjoy shared presence but need control over sound and pace.

**Next step:** Use [[Examples/Research/interview-notes|Interview notes]] to draft two session formats, then test the simpler one.

## Tool-story library

Invite residents to lend an ordinary tool for one evening and attach a short note about when it proved useful. The event becomes a practical inventory and a collection of local stories.

**Constraint:** Do not accept tools that need specialist supervision. A clear intake checklist matters more than a large collection.

**Next step:** Ask the [[Examples/Community/event-plan|repair cafe]] volunteers which five tools people request most often.

## Seasonal route cards

Create small walking cards that pair an accessible route with one observation prompt, such as noticing rain channels, shade, or street-level planting. The [[Examples/Personal/travel-plan|harbor plan]] offers a useful model for route alternatives.

> [!question]
> Would printed cards remain useful after a first walk, or would a reusable field-note template serve people better?
