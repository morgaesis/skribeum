# Kitchen notes

These are working observations for repeatable weekday meals, not polished recipes. Quantities are approximate and each entry records what to change next time.

## Red lentil soup, trial 3

**Base:** onion, carrot, red lentils, tomato, vegetable stock
**Seasoning:** cumin, coriander seed, smoked paprika, lemon

The texture was right after twenty-five minutes at a gentle simmer. Half the soup was blended, which kept some structure without feeling heavy. The smoked paprika dominated the first bowl but softened after the lemon was added.

### Next adjustment

- Use half as much smoked paprika.
- Add the lemon before deciding whether more salt is needed.
- Keep a cup of stock aside for reheating.

## Roasted cabbage and beans

High heat produced crisp edges, but the beans dried out when they went into the oven at the same time as the cabbage. Add them for only the final ten minutes and coat them separately with oil.

| Element | Worked | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cabbage wedges | Browned well | Cut the core slightly thinner |
| White beans | Good flavor | Add later |
| Mustard dressing | Balanced | Make the same amount |
| Toasted crumbs | Useful texture | Add at the table |

> [!tip]
> A useful cooking note records sensory cues, not only elapsed time. Pan shape, ingredient temperature, and burner strength all change the clock.
