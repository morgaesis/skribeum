# Decision log

This note records decisions that affect more than one session. Meeting details remain in [[Examples/Work/meeting-notes|Meeting notes]], while this page captures the durable conclusion and its reasoning.

## D-01: Preserve a clear window-side route

**Status:** Accepted
**Scope:** Cedar Room pilot layout

### Decision

The pilot uses twelve movable chairs in three clusters. The route from the entrance along the windows remains free of furniture, bags, and material storage.

### Reasons

- Interview participants consistently valued predictable movement through the room.
- Twelve chairs leave enough space to change the cluster layout without moving fixed furniture.
- Material tables can sit against the storage wall without crossing the main route.

### Consequences

Registration is capped below the room's maximum seated capacity. The facilitator checks the route during setup and again before the doors open. Extra chairs stay folded unless someone requests one.

## D-02: Use two material stations

**Status:** Trial

### Decision

Duplicate the most-used supplies across two small tables instead of using one central table.

### Measure

Observe whether participants can collect materials without forming a queue across the entrance. Record the result in the next [[Examples/Work/meeting-notes|working-session note]].

> [!important]
> A trial decision has an explicit measure and can be revised without treating the change as a failure.
