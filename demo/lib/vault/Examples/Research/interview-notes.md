# Interview notes: shared workspace visits

**Research question:** What helps someone enter, understand, and use an unfamiliar community workspace?

Three fictional participants described a recent visit to a public workshop. These notes group patterns rather than present verbatim transcripts.

## Participant snapshots

### Participant A

Arrives early to understand the room before it fills. Looks for a place to put a coat, a visible schedule, and a seat with a clear route to the exit. Prefers instructions in writing because spoken introductions are easy to miss.

### Participant B

Enjoys conversation but needs a concrete activity to begin. Finds large supply tables confusing when labels are hidden by other people. Appreciates being told that observation is a valid form of participation.

### Participant C

Often arrives after an event has started. Wants a quiet way to understand what is happening without interrupting the facilitator. Notices whether signs are readable from seated height.

## Patterns

| Need | Evidence across visits | Design response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Predictable arrival | All three looked for orientation cues | Put a short agenda near the entrance |
| Choice of engagement | Two wanted permission to observe | Name observation as an option |
| Clear movement | Two checked routes before sitting | Preserve the window-side path |
| Accessible labels | All relied on written cues | Test signs from seated and standing positions |
