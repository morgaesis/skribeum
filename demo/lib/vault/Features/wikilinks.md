# Wikilinks

Wikilinks connect notes by name. They make it easy to follow an idea without leaving the keyboard.

## Common forms

A direct link points to [[quickstart]]. An alias gives the same destination a conversational label: [[quickstart|return to the guided tour]]. A path can identify a note inside a folder, such as [[Examples/Personal/garden-log]].

Links can target a section: [[Examples/Work/meeting-notes#Open questions|meeting questions]]. The surrounding sentence remains readable even when the destination name is more formal than the phrase it replaces.

## Connections in practice

The [[Examples/Work/project-ideas|project ideas]] note proposes a quiet community workspace. The [[Examples/Research/interview-notes|interview notes]] capture what potential visitors need, and the [[Examples/Work/decision-log|decision log]] records which constraints shape the next prototype.

> [!tip]
> Follow a few links, then use the history controls or quick switcher to move back through the trail.
