# Blockquotes

## Simple blockquote

> A single quoted sentence about the weather over the fjord.

## Multi-line blockquote with markers on every line

> First quoted line about the morning ferry.
> Second quoted line about the afternoon ferry.

## Lazy continuation

> The first line carries the marker
and this lazy line continues the same quote paragraph
with yet another lazy line before the block ends.

## Nested blockquote

> Outer quote level one.
> > Inner quote level two.
> > > Innermost quote level three.

## Nested with lazy continuation

> > A doubly quoted opening line
continues lazily at the inner depth.

## Blockquote containing other blocks

> ### Heading inside a quote
>
> - Quoted bullet one
> - Quoted bullet two
>
> ```text
> fenced code inside a quote
> ```

## Quote interrupted by a blank line becomes two quotes

> First separate quote block.

> Second separate quote block.

## Blockquote without a space after the marker

>Terse quote line with no space after the angle bracket.
