# Hard and soft line breaks

A line ending with two trailing spaces makes a hard break  
so this line renders below it in the same paragraph.

A line ending with a backslash makes a hard break\
and this line follows it.

A line ending with three trailing spaces also breaks   
onto this continuation line.

A soft break is a bare newline
joining two lines into one rendered paragraph
across three source lines.

One trailing space is not a hard break 
so these two lines soft-wrap together.

Hard break inside *emphasized  
text* keeps the emphasis open.
