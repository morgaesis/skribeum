# Callouts

> [!note]
> A plain note callout with a single body line.

> [!note] Callout with a custom title
> Body text under the custom title.

> [!warning]
> A warning callout about wet paint on the railing.

> [!tip] Folded-open callout
> The plus marker requests an expanded default state.

> [!faq]- Folded-closed callout
> The minus marker requests a collapsed default state.

> [!info]+ Explicitly expanded callout
> The plus marker after the type keeps it open.

> [!quote]
> A quote-style callout
> spanning two marked lines.

> [!note]
> A callout with lazy continuation
carried onto an unmarked line.

> [!todo] Callout containing a list
> - First item inside the callout
> - Second item inside the callout

> [!abstract]
> Outer callout body.
> > [!example]
> > Nested inner callout body.

> [!unknown-custom-type]
> A custom type that renderers fall back to a default style for.

> A plain blockquote after the callouts, with [!note] mid-line, is not a callout.
