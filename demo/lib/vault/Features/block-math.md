# Block math

Block math gives an equation its own visual space. This example estimates the total soil volume for three raised beds.

$$
V_{total} = n \times w \times l \times d
$$

With $n = 3$, $w = 1.2\,\text{m}$, $l = 3\,\text{m}$, and $d = 0.35\,\text{m}$:

$$
V_{total} = 3 \times 1.2 \times 3 \times 0.35 = 3.78\,\text{m}^3
$$

An additional ten percent allows for settling:

$$
V_{order} = 3.78 \times 1.10 \approx 4.16\,\text{m}^3
$$

The calculation provides a planning estimate, while [[Examples/Personal/garden-log|Garden log]] records what was actually used.
